## Goal
All randomness in the engine is deterministic and injectable.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `createRng(seed)` returning `next()`, `int(min, max)`, `pick(list)`, `chance(p)`
- [ ] Same seed gives the same sequence; different seeds differ
- [ ] Distribution sanity test (mean of `next()` over 100k draws within tolerance)
- [ ] Rng can be forked into independent streams (e.g. simulation vs commentary)

## Notes
mulberry32 (used in the prototype) is fine.
