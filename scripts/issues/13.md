## Goal
Engine changes cannot silently make football unrealistic.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `npm run test:balance` simulates at least 1000 seeded matches between average teams
- [ ] Asserts: goals per match 2.4 to 3.0, shots per match 20 to 30, total xG within 15% of goals, draw rate 20% to 32%
- [ ] Asserts that a clearly stronger team wins at least 55% of matches
- [ ] Runs in CI job **Balance** in under a minute
- [ ] Prints a short summary table for PR descriptions
