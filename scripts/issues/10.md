## Goal
A deterministic simulation that produces a realistic event log from two team profiles.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `createMatch(input, seed)` with `advanceTo(seconds)` and `events`
- [ ] Transitions: progress, lateral move, turnover, shot, corner (fouls optional)
- [ ] xG from position (distance to goal, centrality); goal probability from xG, finishing and goalkeeping
- [ ] After a goal: kickoff for the conceding team from the centre
- [ ] Same seed and input give an identical event log
- [ ] A stronger team wins more often than a weaker one over many seeds

## Notes
Port the logic from `reference/match-view-prototype.html` (simulation section) as a starting point, then drive it with tests.
