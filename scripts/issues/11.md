## Goal
Matches have proper periods, and the simulation can pause at half-time for user decisions.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Periods: first half, half-time, second half, full time
- [ ] `advanceTo` stops at half-time; `resume()` starts the second half with kickoff for the other team
- [ ] Added time at the end of each half, drawn from the rng
- [ ] Period change events in the log
